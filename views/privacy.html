Privacy
Policy

LAST UPDATED · MAY 6, 2026

DBP Coach is a personalized AI training and nutrition application operated by Durango Bike Project ("we", "DBP"). This policy describes what data we access, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

Data We Access

With your explicit authorization via OAuth, DBP Coach accesses the following data from connected services:

  • WHOOP: recovery score, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep stages and duration, daily strain, workout records, and basic profile data.
  • Strava: activity records including type, duration, distance, heart rate, power, elevation, and route data for activities you have recorded.

We also collect: your email address (for sign-in), name (optional), birthdate (for birthday perks, paid tiers only), bodyweight, training preferences, and benchmarks you provide.

How We Use Your Data

Data is used solely to generate your personalized training and nutrition recommendations and to operate your DBP membership. Your metrics may be sent to AI services for analysis to produce daily prescriptions. Your data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train AI models.

How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored in a private Postgres database hosted on Railway. Access tokens are encrypted at rest. Only DBP Coach systems and authorized DBP staff (for support purposes only) can access your data.

Third-Party Services

DBP Coach transmits data to:

  • Anthropic — for generating recommendations. Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — for billing only. We never see your full payment information. Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Resend — for transactional email. Resend's privacy policy.
  • OpenWeather — for weather context (only your approximate city, not exact coordinates).

Data Retention and Deletion

You can revoke DBP Coach's access to WHOOP at any time at your WHOOP privacy settings and Strava at your Strava settings. To delete your DBP Coach account and all associated data, email ccroberts10@gmail.com. We will permanently delete all data within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

DBP Coach is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of material changes by email. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
