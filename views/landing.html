06 Common Questions
Do I need a WHOOP?
Yes for full Coach functionality — WHOOP recovery is core to the daily prescription. Garmin support is on the roadmap. Strava alone won't unlock Coach yet.
Do I have to live in Durango?
For Coach tier, no — works anywhere. For Elite tier, the rewards (free coffee, service, group rides) require visiting DBP at 225 E 8th Ave. Most Elite members are Durango locals.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your account settings — takes one tap. You keep access through the end of your billing period. No "are you sure" voicemail.
What if I'm not a hardcore cyclist?
Coach is built for anyone who rides — commuters, weekend riders, racers. Set your weekly hours target and primary focus, and the AI calibrates. "Aerobic base" focus is great for newer riders.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your WHOOP and Strava data is used only to personalize your prescription. We don't sell it, share it, or train models on it. Read our Privacy Policy.
Who built this?
Casey Roberts, DBP owner. Real cycling experience, real Durango terrain knowledge, real bike shop service backing the whole thing.
